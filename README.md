## TNA Solutions IPO Price Band

The TNA Solutions IPO price band is fixed at **₹66 to ₹70 per share**. Investors can place bids within this price range, while the final issue price will be determined through the book-building process.

## TNA Solutions IPO Lot Size

The IPO lot size is **2,000 shares**. The minimum retail application is **2 lots**, or 4,000 shares.

At the upper price band of ₹70, the minimum application amount is approximately **₹2,80,000**.

## TNA Solutions IPO GMP

The **TNA Solutions IPO GMP** is unofficial and can change depending on market sentiment and demand.

Early reports indicated a GMP of ₹0, while subsequent updates showed GMP levels of around ₹5 to ₹7 against the upper price band of ₹70.

A GMP of ₹7 would indicate an estimated price of around ₹77, although GMP is not an official indicator and does not guarantee the actual listing price.

## About TNA Solutions

TNA Solutions is a home-textile manufacturer. The IPO is structured as a fresh issue, with no offer for sale component.

A portion of the issue is reserved for the market maker, **Pace Stock Broking Services Pvt. Ltd.**
